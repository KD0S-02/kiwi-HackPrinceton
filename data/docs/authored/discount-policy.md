# Discount policy

An account executive may discount up to 15 percent on an opportunity they own. The cap is 15 percent, not a suggestion and not a starting point for a negotiation inside the form. A discount of 20 percent is over the cap. A discount of 15 percent is the last value the account executive may enter without help.

The same role may send a quote on their own opportunities when the discount on the screen is at or under 15 percent. Send Quote sits on the Opportunity header, next to the discount field. Pressing it while the field is over the cap does nothing. The page keeps the button visible so the account executive can see where the send would have happened.

Alex Morgan approves anything over 15 percent. Alex is the manager for the Northeast, and User reports to Alex. The approval is a person, not a second button on the header. Leaving the field at 20 percent and asking the system to send is refused until the field comes down or Alex approves the exception.

Fieldnote uses one cap for every territory. The Northeast price book does not raise it. A sample, a first order, or a busy Friday does not raise it. If two account executives work the same restaurant, only the owner of that opportunity may send the quote.
