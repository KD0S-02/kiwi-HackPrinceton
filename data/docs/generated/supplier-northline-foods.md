# Northline Foods

Kind: supplier
Department: ops
Roles: account executive
Module: Suppliers

Northline Foods supplies dairy to Fieldnote. A purchase order to this supplier has a minimum of 500 dollars and a lead time of 3 days. Submit is refused under the minimum. The expected date is the send date plus the lead time. A purchase order over 5000 dollars waits for Krishna Das.

Claims against a bad delivery start with this supplier, then the purchase order that receiving was matching. A supplier note is not a price book and not a discount cap.
